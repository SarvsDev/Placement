Mongo DB:

Creating DB: Use <Database name>
Creating Collection: db.createCollection("Name")
Inserting Elements: db.<collectionname>.InsertOne({Name:"Name",Year:2000})
Inserting Multiple Elements: db.<collectionname>.InsertMany([{Name:"Name",Year:2000},{Name:"Name",Year:2000}])
Updating Element: db.<collectionname>.updateOne({Course:"Java"},{$set:{Course:"Android"} } )
Display All element: db.<collectionname>.find()
